03 / The quality test

Made to
outlast.

High-end is not a colour choice. It is the weight of the cloth, the stability of the rib, a photograph that holds its detail and packaging that arrives without apology.

Wash
Five cycles; maximum 5% shrinkage target
Print
No cracking, bleeding or weak black density
Finish
Clean embroidery, seam alignment and minimal plastic

Prototype only / physical sample required before checkout

04 / Release discipline

Do not add
for the sake of it.

  1. 01Approve the seven core samples before public checkout.
  2. 02The hoodie must prove its wash, fit and margin before release.
  3. 03Release the postcard set with a real field note, event or collector order—not as filler.
  4. 04Keep Faire for wholesale only after direct-to-collector product quality is proven.

05 / Production rule

No stock.
No false scarcity.

Different objects may require different specialist production routes. Every SKU is verified for its material, the buyer’s destination and the actual quality of its finished sample—not selected from a catalogue alone.

Made-to-order production reduces inventory risk; it does not remove the need for quality control. Every public product requires an approved physical sample, a confirmed landed margin and a tested delivery route.
