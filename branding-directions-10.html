Temple identity system · Ten complete directions

Ten possible Domus Cultura worlds.

Each direction combines a temple emblem, wordmark, typography, editorial language, photography, apparel and packaging system.

Universal logo rules

  1. Use the full lockup when the audience may not know the brand.
  2. Use the emblem alone only when Domus Cultura is already identified.
  3. Clear space: at least one-quarter of the temple height.
  4. Minimum emblem: 16 mm print, 18 mm embroidery, 24 px digital.
  5. Minimum full lockup: 28 mm print or 140 px digital.
  6. Never stretch, rotate, outline, crop or add effects.
  7. Preserve the exact spelling DOMUS CULTURA.
  8. Test every master in one colour before applying a palette.
  9. Garment bases: white, black, grey, dark green and dark blue.
  10. Supplier artwork must use exact vector geometry and outlined type.

These boards are strategic visual concepts. After one direction is selected, the logo geometry and proprietary wordmark will be rebuilt as production-ready SVG masters.
