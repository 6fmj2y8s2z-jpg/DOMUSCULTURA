Domus Cultura / Merch identity round 01

HERITAGE
IN MOTION.

Six original, embroidery-first directions for a cultural uniform. They borrow the confidence of heritage sport and equestrian insignia without copying any existing brand language: architecture, movement, procession and a house built for culture.

01 / Clear at 18 mm

Every mark is built from broad, one-colour strokes that can be digitised for caps, chest embroidery and woven labels.

02 / A real house symbol

The shared language is architecture, not a borrowed animal or sports icon: porticos, thresholds, flags and columns define Domus Cultura.

03 / Existing wordmark preserved

These symbols sit beside the website’s DOMUS CULTURA typography. The wordmark remains the public-facing anchor until a final identity is selected.

Before supplier upload.

These are original SVG vector masters, so they are scale-independent and superior to 600 DPI raster references for embroidery digitisation. Once one direction is selected, it still needs a physical stitch-out, final optical correction and trademark clearance before it is used on products.
