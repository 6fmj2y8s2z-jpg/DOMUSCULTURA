Placement proposals for the two strongest new identity directions. These are visual sampling instructions, not production approvals: every embroidery location needs a physical stitch-out on the exact garment before release.

A / Core option

M01 House Pennant

The preferred core identity. A direct house emblem with a forward flag gives the collection a confident heritage-sport feeling without borrowing another brand’s visual assets.

Chest: 50 mm wide
Cap: 42 mm wide
Back wordmark: 240 mm wide
Thread: cream on dark bases, black on grey

B / Artistic option

M05 Threaded Gate

The more distinct cultural option. Its repeated line carries the feeling of stitching, architecture and passage, making it especially strong for tonal embroidery and labels.

Chest: 46 mm wide
Cap: 38 mm wide
Back wordmark: 220 mm wide
Thread: tonal or cream, maximum two thread colours

Sample only after these checks.

Use the current website wordmark with the proposed emblem; do not mix multiple marks on one product. Keep the garment palette to black, dark navy, hunter green, grey and white.

  • Approve the exact emblem at a real 18 mm embroidery test before digitisation.
  • Check puffing, thread tension, registration and legibility after five washes.
  • For supplier files, convert the final wordmark to outlines and request a DST, PES or EXP stitch file after approval.
  • Keep M01 and M05 separate for testing. Select one final system before public product images are made.
