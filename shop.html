Questions, answered

THE DETAILS
MATTER.

When will the collection be available?

The password-protected shop is being prepared for private testing. Public release follows approved physical samples, confirmed production routes and a final quality check.

Where are objects made and shipped from?

We work with selected European production partners. The exact fulfilment location and delivery estimate are shown on each released product page before checkout.

How do returns work?

Return terms will be published with the live collection. Made-to-order objects are assessed individually, while damaged or incorrect items are replaced or refunded through our support team.

Are the photographs limited editions?

Yes. Photography editions are separate from apparel and objects. Each image draws from one shared edition of 100 across its three unframed sizes and is supplied with a numbered certificate after release.
