Artist submissions / Rolling programme

Bring your work
into the house.

Domus Cultura welcomes considered proposals from artists, photographers, writers, sound practitioners and independent cultural projects.

Selected practices may be featured through an interview, studio visit, journal story, event, collaboration or limited-edition proposal.

What we look for

A clear practice.
A real point of view.

01

Contemporary relevance

Work that speaks to life now—socially, materially or imaginatively.

02

Visual strength

A coherent body of work with images suitable for thoughtful editorial presentation.

03

Human context

A process, place or lived experience worth opening to a wider public.

04

Mutual fit

A meaningful connection to art, architecture, photography, sound or public culture.
