# Domus Cultura — 20 logo directions

All concepts include:

- an editable SVG master at 1200 × 600 viewBox;
- a transparent PNG at 3600 × 1800 pixels with 600 DPI metadata;
- a 6 × 3 inch maximum print area at the supplied 600 DPI;
- exact `DOMUS CULTURA` lettering rendered in the PNG export.

The SVG files remain resolution-independent. Their live text uses macOS system typefaces; the 600-DPI PNGs are the locked production references for supplier sampling. A print supplier should convert the final selected SVG typography to outlines before production.

### 21 / Vivid House

This hybrid combines the stacked authority of 01, the stencil structure of 10, the constructivist geometry of 13 and the softer overlapping forms of 20. Its dominant vibrant yellow is supported by saturated red, pink and a substantial hunter-green field. The stencil rails frame the wordmark without obscuring any lettering. Use the full-colour version for campaigns, apparel and packaging; prepare a simplified one-colour production variant before embroidery.

## Production note

Do not send all 20 designs to production. Select one primary wordmark, one compact monogram and at most two campaign marks. Test embroidery at the supplier's actual stitch size, and screen-print each selected mark before opening checkout.
