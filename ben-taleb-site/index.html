“I photograph the moment when a place stops being background and becomes a presence.”

The city is never still. Even its most permanent structures change through light, weather, memory and the bodies passing through them.

The camera slows that movement without fixing it completely. Each photograph leaves space for the viewer to enter, question and continue the image.
