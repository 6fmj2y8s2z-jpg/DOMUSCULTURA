Ben Taleb develops a photographic practice around architecture, movement and everyday encounters. His images move between precise spatial compositions and fleeting human situations.

Working primarily in black and white, he looks for the point where buildings become theatre, reflections become abstraction and ordinary gestures reveal the character of a place.

His ongoing project Good Morning World studies how people move through constructed environments—and how light, weather and chance briefly transform those environments.

To look is to participate.

My work is about attention: noticing what is already there, understanding how spaces shape behaviour, and finding intimacy inside scale.

A photograph should not close a moment. It should make that moment available again—open to memory, projection and another way of seeing.
