Ongoing photographic project

Good Morning
World

Good Morning World is a visual diary of architecture, public space and human presence. The work searches for moments in which scale becomes intimate, reflections rearrange reality, and an ordinary passage becomes a small piece of theatre.

The project remains open: a growing sequence of observations made across places rather than a closed series tied to one city.
