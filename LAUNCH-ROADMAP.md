# Domus Cultura — 24-month revenue roadmap

Operating targets, not guarantees. Prioritise cash flow, proof of demand and a recognisable cultural point of view.

## Recommended stack

- Shopify in CHF for merchandise, checkout, payments, inventory and the editorial journal.
- Hahnemühle My Art Registry 2.0 for certified, limited Ben Taleb fine-art editions: print-on-demand through certified studios, edition registry, certificate and matching hologram.
- Link each Ben Taleb product page to its verified My Art Registry record; keep edition numbering consistent across both systems.
- Prodigi remains the fallback/comparison supplier for Hahnemühle Photo Rag / German Etching if My Art Registry cannot support a required country, format or fulfilment workflow.
- Compare physical samples from Prodigi and Printful for apparel and objects.
- Three unframed print sizes; 100 copies maximum per image across all sizes; numbered certificate.
- Launch price bands: small CHF 150–200, medium CHF 300–450, large CHF 600–800.
- SoundCloud embeds for a weekly 45–60 minute Juice+ programme.
- Instagram for discovery, email for ownership, events for trust, store for conversion.

## Zero-inventory operating model

- No speculative stock: every sellable item is produced only after a paid order.
- Fine-art photography: My Art Registry routes certified Hahnemühle production through its studio network.
- Apparel and objects: an approved Shopify print-on-demand partner fulfils T-shirts, heavyweight sweatshirts, caps, mugs and totes.
- Paper goods: postcards and small print sets are printed on demand or sold through short, fully pre-sold releases.
- Shopify remains the central storefront; each order is automatically routed to the appropriate production partner.
- Samples are the only physical items Domus Cultura buys before launch, because colour, textile weight, fit and packaging must be approved.
- Products are added slowly: one tested supplier and one approved sample per product category before public sale.

## Months 2–3 — prove demand

- One Juice+ mix and one journal article per week.
- One photography edition per month; no oversized catalogue.
- One gallery/studio visit or listening event per month.
- Goal: 100 qualified subscribers, 10 customers, CHF 3k–8k cumulative revenue.

## Months 4–6 — make it repeatable

- Drop 02 based on the best size and price from Drop 01.
- Add two merch products only after sample approval.
- Pitch five local venues weekly for a pop-up or Juice+ night.
- Test one 30–50 unit artist collaboration with a written revenue split.
- Add B2B art placement, photography and cultural-content offers.
- Goal: CHF 12k–25k cumulative revenue.

## Months 7–12 — establish the house

- Quarterly drops, each with an event and editorial story.
- Ben Taleb commission package at CHF 1,500–4,000.
- Creative/cultural direction retainers at CHF 900–2,500/month.
- Collector CRM with interests, purchases and follow-up dates.
- Year-one objective: CHF 45k–75k.

## Months 13–18 — scale the winners

- Retire weak products; expand the strongest 20%.
- Two artist collaborations with clear rights and edition agreements.
- Corporate art placement for offices, hotels and restaurants.
- One recurring venue partnership for Domus Cultura Sound Nights.
- Six-month objective: CHF 45k–70k.

## Months 19–24 — grow beyond the founder

- Pre-order funded Domus Cultura zine.
- Two-day pop-up combining editions, talks, music and commissions.
- Wholesale terms for museum shops and concept stores.
- Collector tier with previews, visits and first refusal.
- Year-two objective: CHF 100k–160k, with a path toward CHF 200k combined annual income when services and commissions are included.

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday: pipeline, fulfilment, finances and metrics.
- Tuesday: photography or product creation.
- Wednesday: article, edit or artist conversation.
- Thursday: outreach, partnerships and proposals.
- Friday: Juice+ mix and publishing.
- Saturday: gallery, atelier and event fieldwork.
- Sunday: archive, select and plan.
