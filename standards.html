Domus Cultura / How objects enter the house

NOTHING
WITHOUT
A PROOF.

We make editions and useful cultural objects slowly: with original work, accurate material specifications and a physical sample before a product is offered.

01 / The rule

Made to order.
Made accountable.

We do not build artificial scarcity or speculative inventory. An item enters the collection only when its material, image, production route and landed quality have been checked together. Until then, it remains a study—not a promise to sell.

01

Original image

Every photographic work originates with Ben Taleb or an invited artist whose rights and context are clear. Production uses the original high-resolution master, never a compressed website preview.

02

Three honest views

Every object needs a front, reverse and close material view. Room images show scale; they do not replace a view of the real object.

03

Sample before release

We check tonal range, fit, print density, embroidery, packaging and delivery before publication. A catalogue image does not qualify a product.

04

One route, tested

Public availability follows a verified production and delivery route for the destination—not an assumption that one supplier can make every object well.

02 / Fine-art photography

One photograph.
One shared count.

Looking Up / Edition 001 is planned as an unframed photograph on Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308 gsm, 100% cotton, with archival pigment inks. The three formats—30 × 40, 45 × 60 and 60 × 80 cm—belong to a single edition of 100 in total, not 100 copies per size.

03 / Objects and apparel

Material is
the message.

The permanent line is deliberately small. It privileges weight, durability and a restrained mark over endless variations.

Heavyweight T-shirt220–240 gsm target

Combed, ring-spun organic cotton; side seams and a substantial collar rib.

Crewneck / hoodie320–350 gsm target

Dense brushed interior, stable rib. Hoodies require a double-layer hood and clean hardware.

Embroidered cap260 gsm twill target

Six-panel cotton construction, direct embroidery and a metal or brass closure.

Canvas tote280–310 gsm target

Organic canvas or denim with reinforced handles; no lightweight promotional bags.

Postcard set350 gsm FSC stock target

Original images on uncoated or silk-matte paper, finished to a museum-shop standard.

04 / What we test

A quality claim
has to survive use.

  1. 01Measure garment dimensions and inspect the parcel before the first wash.
  2. 02Wash textiles five times; check shrinkage, rib recovery, seam twist, pilling, print and embroidery.
  3. 03Load-test tote handles; test ceramic print alignment and dishwasher durability where applicable.
  4. 04Record delivery, packaging, Swiss landed cost and replacement handling before an item can be approved.
